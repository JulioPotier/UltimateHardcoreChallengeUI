# Ultimate Hardcore Challenge UI

An add-on for **World of Warcraft Classic Era** that helps you set up and track a **personal hardcore-style challenge**: what gear you allow, where you travel, professions, talents, extras like bags and conveniences—and how much in-game gold you’re “spending” to unlock options as you play.

Everything is organised in tabs so you can see your rules at a glance, adjust choices as your run evolves, and keep your character’s picks saved between sessions.

## Who it’s for

Players who enjoy **self-imposed difficulty**—whether you stream it, guildmates hold you accountable, or you just like a clearer picture of “what counts” for your own run.

## Highlights

- **Equipment & gear rules** — slot-by-slot allowances and gear quality so your character sheet matches what you promised yourself.
- **Weapons, services & quality-of-life options** — group the bits that aren’t vanilla gear slots in one place.
- **Professions & talents** — lock in how you skill and talent your character within the challenge.
- **Zones & dungeons** — pick where you’re allowed to quest, explore, run dungeons or battlegrounds, with level cues where it helps.
- **Settings & money tracking** — optional ways to coordinate rules with gold and reminders; reset or support links when you need them.

## Getting started in-game

Install the add-on through your usual **AddOns** setup, enable it on the character select screen, then log in. Assign a key under **Escape → Options → Key Bindings → Other** (look for Ultimate Hardcore Challenge) to open the window whenever you need it.

Your choices are remembered **per character** so alts don’t overwrite each other’s challenge.
